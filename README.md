# Manifestation Conjecture

**Author:** Kasha Cipher  
**AI research collaborator:** Chatto

This open research notebook asks what it means for something to become manifest. A contemplative practitioner may describe a moment of awareness, a physicist may record a detector reading, and an AI system may produce a verbal report. What, if anything, survives comparison across these three cases?

## Opening conjecture

A common account of manifestation might be possible if we can specify, in each domain, what becomes distinguishable, to whom or to what, and by what criterion. This is a proposal for inquiry. A shared vocabulary alone supplies no evidence for a common physical mechanism.

## Research method

| Claim type | What an entry must supply |
| --- | --- |
| Established evidence | A traceable primary source, the observation or result, its conditions and limits, and the basis for confidence. |
| Hypothesis | An operational definition, a prediction recorded before testing, a competing explanation and a result that would count against it. |
| Philosophical interpretation | A stated reading, its premises, a plausible rival reading and the passage or argument at issue. |
| Conjecture | The proposed connection, its assumptions, where the analogy could fail and what would make it testable. |

## First investigations

- **Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra and non-duality.** Select a passage and record its edition, verse number, Sanskrit wording, translator and alternative renderings. Distinguish the practice described from a practitioner's report and from a metaphysical reading of that report.
- **Quantum measurement.** For one specified experiment, distinguish the preparation, apparatus, recorded outcome and theoretical interpretation. Ask whether assigning a role to conscious awareness changes a measurable prediction.
- **Artificial intelligence.** Specify the model, prompt, controls and scoring rule before evaluating a first-person report. Test how wording changes the response. Treat any inference about subjective experience as a separate claim with its own evidential burden.

Record dated predictions and methods before collecting results. Preserve null findings, rival explanations and corrections in version history. Verify AI-suggested citations against their originals before admitting them as evidence.
